WB Series 0.1 mg Internal Calibration Analytical Balance

An analytical balance with a key internal automatic calibration function for more convenient, stable use.

Overview

Equipped with a standard RS232C data interface for connection to a computer, printer or other device, with a key internal automatic calibration function as its most important feature. A large backlit LCD screen, five-surface transparent glass windshield and a new-generation electromagnetic gauge transducer provide stable, reliable high precision, with automatic fault detection, auto-calibration and overload protection.

Applications

  • Laboratory mass measurement where convenient internal recalibration is required
  • Routine analytical weighing with automatic fault detection and overload protection
  • Applications benefiting from a user-friendly, five-surface glass windshield
